# LinkedIn post draft

I turned my personal finance workflow into a reusable agent skill.

It creates one Control Center for budgets, spending, subscriptions, debt, savings goals, and investment summaries, even when the data comes from several sources.

Every important fact keeps its source, timestamp, and uncertainty, so multiple agents cannot silently overwrite one another.

It can prepare a cancellation, payment, transfer, or account-link recommendation, but external actions stay approval-gated.

I made the repo public with generic adapters, fictional data, privacy rules, tests, and both ChatGPT-style and Claude-style instructions.
